# FOX CINEMA artwork

- `*-original.webp`: original cinematic illustrations generated for this reconstruction (not still frames from the user's reference videos).
- `*-original.svg`: original vector offer/experience artwork drawn for this project.
- `poster-fallback.svg`: original FOX-style generic placeholder when a real official film poster cannot be retrieved or a host disallows hotlinking.
- Film posters are **not copied into this repository**. Curated external promotional poster URLs are listed with attribution/source URLs in `public/catalog.js`.

Film images remain the property of their respective owners. Obtain the correct artwork licenses before commercial production usage, or use a properly licensed poster feed. This is a noncommercial demonstration of a fictional cinema brand; no live ticket availability is asserted.
